Zyrox Tech Privacy Policy

Zyrox Tech creates digital products, gaming experiences, and modern technology solutions with a focus on performance, realistic user experiences, and secure service delivery. This privacy policy explains how personal data may be collected, used, protected, and shared when interacting with our applications, products, and website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information such as:

  • Log information, including IP address, access times, browser or app details, and device information.
  • Device data such as model, operating system, language settings, and time zone.
  • Usage information, such as gameplay activity, feature interactions, scores, progress, and account-related behavior.
  • Purchase information for in-app products or services, when relevant.
  • Identifiers such as advertising IDs, app account IDs, and profile information from supported third-party sign-ins.

2. How We Use Information

We use personal and non-personal data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our products and services.
  • Improve gameplay, software stability, and overall user experience.
  • Respond to support requests and customer communication.
  • Send product updates, service notices, and relevant messaging where permitted.
  • Analyze performance, detect issues, and improve business decisions.
  • Deliver personalized experiences and relevant advertising where applicable.

3. Data from Third-Party Services

If you sign in using a third-party account or platform, we may access public profile details as permitted by that service, such as display name and email address. We may also receive information from analytics or advertising partners used to improve our products and optimize campaigns.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share data with trusted service providers such as analytics, advertising, support, or hosting partners to help operate and improve our services. We may also disclose information when required by law, regulation, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of users and the company.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as required to provide our services, manage legitimate business needs, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect our systems and users. Some usage data may be retained for shorter periods or deleted when no longer needed.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our products are not intended for children under the age of 13. If we discover that information from a child under the applicable age threshold has been collected, we will delete it promptly and prevent further collection where possible.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or alteration. However, no digital service can guarantee complete security, and users should take appropriate precautions when sharing information online.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data. You may also have the right to withdraw consent for certain processing activities where consent is required. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.
